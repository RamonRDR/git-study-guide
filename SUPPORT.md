# Support guide

[English](SUPPORT.md) · [Português](docs/localized/SUPPORT.pt-BR.md) · [Español](docs/localized/SUPPORT.es.md)

## Choose the right channel

- [Learning question](https://github.com/RamonRDR/git-study-guide/issues/new?template=learning-question.md): Ask about a published lesson.
- [Content or example bug](https://github.com/RamonRDR/git-study-guide/issues/new?template=bug-report.md): Report a reproducible non-security issue.
- [Content suggestion](https://github.com/RamonRDR/git-study-guide/issues/new?template=content-suggestion.md): Propose a focused educational improvement.
- [Translation improvement](https://github.com/RamonRDR/git-study-guide/issues/new?template=translation-improvement.md): Correct meaning or clarity across locales.
- [Private contact request (public issue)](https://github.com/RamonRDR/git-study-guide/issues/new?template=private-contact-request.md): Request a private channel only; do not disclose sensitive information.

Use the security policy and code of conduct for sensitive issues. Do not post private information. Questions may be written in EN, PT-BR or ES; preserve exact technical identifiers.

## What support covers

Published lessons, reproducing documented examples, broken links, translations, contribution requirements and roadmap-aligned suggestions. Provide path, expected and observed behavior, minimal fictional example, Git version/OS/shell and checks already tried when relevant. Never upload private repos or credentials.

## Limits

Best-effort community support; no guaranteed response, implementation, urgent support, account recovery, unrelated project debugging, completion of assignments, personalized mentoring or official product support. Use official vendor channels for platform account problems. Issues may close for duplicates, missing safe reproduction, sensitive content or out-of-scope requests. Respectful follow-up is welcome.
