# Git Study Guide Constitution

[English](constitution.md) · [Português](constitution.pt-BR.md) · [Español](constitution.es.md)

Foundation approved and integrated via PR #1 on 2026-11-25. Subsequent changes require review and explicit maintainer approval.

> Study. Understand. Practice.

## Principles

1. Technical accuracy and verifiable evidence take precedence over speed; verify evolving behavior against official sources and disclose untested claims.
2. Educational clarity: explain what, why, effects, prerequisites and risks before practice; do not assume programming experience.
3. Keep the guide language-neutral, independent, public and free; no paid prerequisite, paid infrastructure or additional financial investment.
4. English is primary; all public educational, engineering, architecture and governance documents must be conceptually aligned in EN, pt-BR and es in the same PR. Names, code, technical comments, IDs, schema keys, branches and commits remain English. Preserve the original MIT license; translate explanations separately.
5. Develop incrementally through SDD; establish requirements, plans, tasks, acceptance criteria and ADRs before implementation. Keep scope focused and architecture extensible without premature abstraction.
6. Use original, fictional, generic, non-confidential examples. Never publish secrets, private data, employer/client information or proprietary material; superficial anonymization is insufficient.
7. Make the repository the sole authoritative content source. Learning Hub consumes approved immutable revisions; no website implementation or required dependency on another guide.
8. Teach manual, independent Git practice and reasoning. AI may tutor or assist development but never substitutes understanding, deterministic verification or human responsibility; no mandatory AI subscription or semantic AI grading.
9. Practice risky operations only in disposable repositories; teach effects, preconditions, recovery and limits. Never target the guide itself; isolate tests, avoid privileged execution of untrusted submissions and use least privilege.
10. Preserve authorship, license, history and contributor credit. Ramon Estevez Rodriguez (Ramon Rodriguez) owns strategic, educational and editorial direction, visual identity and final approval. ChatGPT and Codex may assist implementation and review; the human maintainer remains accountable. Do not imply official endorsement.
11. Every change requires an up-to-date focused branch, PR, applicable validation, review of the latest revision, addressed findings and explicit maintainer approval before squash merge. Never modify main directly, bypass protections, fabricate approvals or confuse requested review with completed review.
12. Disclose limitations and unrun checks, including unavailable Codex review or CI. Do not claim completion without evidence; change constitutional principles only via a reviewed specification PR.

AGENTS.md defines repository contributor guidance. Conflicts require maintainer resolution. Engineering sprint numbers are distinct from curriculum order.
