# Code of conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Português](docs/localized/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md) · [Español](docs/localized/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

## Scope and expected behavior

All participants in repository issues, PRs, reviews, discussions and project-related communication should be respectful, patient and technically honest. Welcome beginners and all three languages; give constructive corrections without humiliation, respect privacy and attribution, and distinguish facts from uncertainty.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, hate, sexualized harassment, personal attacks, hostile gatekeeping, impersonation, plagiarism, doxxing, demands for private information, malicious contributions, intentional fabrication and retaliation are prohibited. Disagreement is welcome when focused on the work, not the person.

## Reporting and confidentiality

Use a private contact method voluntarily published by the [maintainer](https://github.com/RamonRDR), if available. Otherwise use the [private contact request](https://github.com/RamonRDR/git-study-guide/issues/new?template=private-contact-request.md) to ask for a channel only; the issue itself is PUBLIC. Do not include names, incident details, evidence, screenshots, contact details or other sensitive information. If even a public request would expose someone, do not post; use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abuse. In the eventual private channel, share only relevant context and evidence. Confidentiality is best effort, not absolute.

## Enforcement

The maintainer evaluates context, impact, repetition and safety. Proportionate measures may include clarification, removal of harmful material, warning, temporary restriction, permanent exclusion or escalation to GitHub or appropriate authorities. Good-faith incomplete reports are not punished; intentionally abusive reporting and retaliation may be violations. If the maintainer is implicated, use independent GitHub reporting rather than relying solely on the maintainer. No guaranteed response time.
